Questions

Answers before you call.

A quick overview of what customers usually ask before scheduling.

Do I need dance experience to take a class?

No. Urbanity offers open-level and beginner-friendly options. If you are new, start with a class that matches your comfort level and contact the studio if you need help choosing.

Do I need to bring a partner?

A partner is not required for most partner dance classes. Classes are designed to help dancers learn, rotate, and connect in a comfortable group environment.

How do I sign up for class?

Use the class calendar, select your class, choose your purchase option, and complete registration before 3 PM on the day of class.
