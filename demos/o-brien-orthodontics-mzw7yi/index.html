Before your visit

Quick answers for appointments, location, and care questions.

When should my child see an orthodontist?

Many families schedule an orthodontic evaluation when adult teeth are coming in or when a dentist notices crowding, spacing, or bite concerns. O'Brien Orthodontics can help determine whether treatment is needed now or should be monitored.

Do you see adults for orthodontic treatment?

Yes. Adults can schedule an orthodontic consultation to discuss alignment, bite concerns, braces, clear aligner possibilities, and retainer needs.
